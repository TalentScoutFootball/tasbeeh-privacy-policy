Privacy Policy – Tasbeeh

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tasbeeh mobile application, developed by Tasbeeh Studio, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Used by the App

Location: The app uses your device location to calculate local prayer times. Location coordinates and related location preferences may be saved locally on your device to remember your settings.

App preferences: The app may store settings such as adhan preferences, Quran reading position, and bookmarks locally on your device.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent management technology to display advertisements where permitted.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information, in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

For more information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob policies and information

3. Location Services

The app may use Android location and geocoding services to determine your location and display relevant prayer times. These services may process location information according to their respective policies.

4. Notifications

The app can schedule local notifications for prayer times and adhan reminders. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

App preferences and saved reading information are stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion policies of those providers.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the app, please contact us.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
